App Name: CalmBoundaries
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: Digital Wellness / Screen Time
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free + $5.99/month Pro

Description:
A smart screen-time blocker that turns lockouts into interactive micro-breathing or stretching mini-games that users must complete before accessing distracting apps.

Key Features:
- Select apps to block (social media, games, news)
- Set block schedules (morning focus hours, work hours)
- When attempting to open blocked app: must complete a micro-activity
- Micro-activities: 4-7-8 breathing exercise, 30-second stretch, 10 jumping jacks count
- Activity difficulty scales with how many times you've tried to bypass today
- Block streak rewards for successful days
- Weekly screen time reduction stats
- Emergency unlock (limited per day, requires typing reason)
- Focus sessions: block all at once for deep work periods

Target Audience: People fighting social media addiction, productivity seekers, parents for teens
Competitors: Digital Wellbeing, Freedom, AppBlock, One Sec
Unique Value: Replaces mindless app-opening with a mindful micro-practice instead of hard blocking
